# Vision docs

Public-facing narrative. Why PhantomOS exists, what it argues, what it ships next.

## Files

- **`prisms.md`** : eight framings of PhantomOS, each for a specific audience. Internal calibration snippets, used one at a time across the product surface.
- **`manifesto.md`** : the thesis. The problem, the distinction (encoding is not logging), the method, the proof, the risks. First-party, opinionated, durable. No third-party market statistics.
- **`roadmap.md`** : what ships next and why. Versioned.

## Who this is for

Anyone evaluating PhantomOS : investors, operators still deciding, consultants curious about the method, journalists, future contributors. Also the maintainer in six months, reading their own thesis to check it still holds.

## Conventions

Vision docs are the only place in the doc surface where name-dropping is allowed (in `manifesto.md`, as scale analogues, never as proof). Prisms and roadmap stay name-drop-free. The only numbers cited anywhere are the system's own, precise and verifiable. Opinionated, narrative, durable over clever.
